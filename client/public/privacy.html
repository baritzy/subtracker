Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2025

SubTracker ("we," "our," or "the app") is a subscription management app developed by Baritzy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use SubTracker on Android.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address for the purpose of identifying your account. We do not access your Gmail messages, inbox, or any other Google account data.
  • Subscription data: Information about subscriptions you enter manually in the app, including service names, renewal dates, and costs.
  • Device information: Basic device identifiers used for app functionality and crash reporting.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To store and display your subscription data across sessions.
  • To provide direct cancellation links for your subscriptions.
  • To deliver app updates and improvements.

3. Data Storage

Your subscription data is stored securely on servers hosted by Supabase (cloud database provider). Data may be stored on servers located in South Korea (ap-northeast-2 region). We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law or to deliver the core functionality of the app (e.g., authentication via Google Sign-In).

5. Google Sign-In

We use Google Sign-In solely to identify and authenticate your account. We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Contacts, or any other Google services beyond basic profile information (name and email address).

6. In-App Purchases

SubTracker offers an optional one-time purchase ("Premium Unlock") processed through Google Play. We do not store or have access to your payment information. All payment processing is handled by Google.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Upon receiving a valid deletion request, we will remove your data from our servers within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

SubTracker is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, please contact us at: baritzy@gmail.com
